1. What is a Computer?

A computer is an electronic device that accepts data, processes it, stores information, and produces results. Computers are used in schools, offices, hospitals, banks, and homes.

2. Main Parts of a Computer

  • Monitor
  • CPU (Central Processing Unit)
  • Keyboard
  • Mouse
  • RAM (Random Access Memory)
  • Hard Disk or SSD

3. Uses of Computers

  • Education and online learning
  • Office work
  • Internet browsing
  • Gaming and entertainment
  • Banking and business
  • Programming and website development

4. Types of Computers

  • Desktop Computer
  • Laptop Computer
  • Tablet
  • Supercomputer
  • Mainframe Computer
